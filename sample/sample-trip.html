▸ Methodology & sources 8 papers, every constant traceable

This plan was generated by a circadian phase-shift algorithm that applies eleven rules grounded in primary sleep-medicine literature. Every numerical constant traces back to a specific study.

Westward delays are easier than eastward advances. Human τ ≈ 24.2 h, slightly long, so the body drifts later naturally. With full intervention you can shift ~1.5 h/day west, ~1.0 h/day east. That's why SF outbound recovery is 4 days but the return is closer to 6.

Light is more powerful than melatonin. Properly-timed bright outdoor light shifts the clock 2–3× more than 0.5 mg melatonin per dose. Melatonin's job is to make sleep happen at the right time so the next day's light dose lands in the right window.

Caffeine is for alertness, not for shifting. ~40 min phase delay from a strong dose, half the magnitude of evening bright light. Cutoffs exist to protect sleep, not to drive the clock.

Primary literature: Khalsa et al. 2003 (light PRC) · Burgess et al. 2010 (0.5 mg melatonin PRC) · Zeitzer et al. 2000 (light dose-response) · Burke et al. 2015 (caffeine pharmacology) · Eastman & Burgess 2009 (operational protocols) · Borbély 1982 (two-process sleep model) · Burgess 2014 + Kantermann 2015 (DLMO from chronotype).
